MENDELU canteen and menu

The MENDELU canteens are run by the Halls and Canteens Administration (SKM). On the Zemědělská campus there is Výdejna X and the pizzeria in building O; another canteen is at the JAK halls and one more at Akademie.

Where to find the menu, when the canteens are open and how to pay.

1The menu in reIS

  1. In Calendar, under today, tap Menu.

  2. Switch between Výdejna X, JAK, Akademie (KA), S-klub and the pizzeria.

In the reIS browser extension, the menu sits by each day in the weekly timetable.

On a computer: the extension for Chrome · Firefox.

2Opening hours

  • Výdejna X (Zemědělská 1, building X): Mon–Thu 11–15, Fri 11–13:30
  • Pizzeria O (building O, ground floor): Mon–Thu 8–15, pizza 10:30–14:30; Fri 8–13, pizza until 13
  • Menza JAK (JAK halls, Kohoutova 5): Mon–Thu 11–14, Fri 11–13:30
  • Výdejna Akademie (tř. Generála Píky 7): Mon–Thu 11:30–14, Fri 11:30–13:30
  • Lednice canteen (Valtická 538): Mon–Fri 11–13

According to SKM, checked on 27 November 2026. Hours may differ in the holidays and the exam period.

3How to pay

  • With your ISIC. You pay from the prepaid account on your ISIC, which every MENDELU student has.
  • Top up the account in WebISKAM by card, direct debit or bank transfer, or at a hall reception or canteen cash desk. 500 CZK is recommended, 200 CZK is the minimum. Guide: ISIC top-up.
  • Student price: a main dish is about 90 CZK, pizza from 80 CZK (November 2026). Up to 9 meals a day at the student price.

MENDELU canteen: frequently asked questions

Where do I find the MENDELU canteen menu?

In reIS under each day in Calendar, for Výdejna X, JAK, Akademie, S-klub and the pizzeria. On the web it is the SKM menu page, usually about a week ahead.

When is the canteen on Zemědělská open?

Výdejna X in building X is open Mon–Thu 11–15 and Fri 11–13:30. The pizzeria in building O bakes pizza Mon–Thu 10:30–14:30.

How do I pay in the MENDELU canteen?

With your ISIC, from the prepaid account. Top it up in WebISKAM, at a hall reception or at a canteen cash desk. See ISIC top-up.

Do I have to order meals in advance?

Not in Brno: just come and choose. In Lednice meals are ordered in advance through WebISKAM.

More questions (8)

How much is lunch in the canteen?

For students about 90 CZK for a main dish, soup 12 CZK, pizza from 80 CZK (November 2026). Staff and the public pay a different price.

Where is the pizzeria and when is it open?

Pizzeria O is on the ground floor of building O on Zemědělská. Open Mon–Thu 8–15, pizza 10:30–14:30, Friday until 13.

How many meals can I buy at the student price?

Up to 9 meals a day.

Can I eat in the canteen if I study at another university?

Yes. SKM activates your ISIC on request and you top up the account like MENDELU students.

Are allergens and vegetarian meals marked?

Allergens are numbered next to each dish on the SKM menu page. There is usually a vegetarian dish, but it is not labelled as such.

What about money left on my account when I finish?

Withdraw it at the Menza JAK office (Kohoutova 5), or write to skm.koleje@mendelu.cz.

Is S-klub in reIS too?

Yes, S-klub has its own tab in the reIS menu.

Who do I contact about the canteen?

Výdejna X: skm.menza@mendelu.cz, phone 545 135 155.
