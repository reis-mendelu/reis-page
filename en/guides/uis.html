UIS MENDELU

UIS (the University Information System, is.mendelu.cz) is MENDELU's study system: timetable, exams, grades, courses and study documents.

How to log in to UIS, and how to see the same things clearly in reIS, on your phone and in your browser.

1Logging in to UIS

  1. Open is.mendelu.cz and click Log in to the Personal administration of UIS.

  2. Enter your UIS login and password.

  3. First-year? You'll find your login and password in the e-application the day after enrolment.

  4. Can't get in? Reset your password on the password reset page. If that doesn't help, write to uis@mendelu.cz.

2It's clearer in reIS

reIS takes your data from UIS and shows it in one app. You log in with the same login and password. reIS is made by MENDELU students, it isn't an official MENDELU app, and your data stays on your device.

My experience: why UIS takes me so long

By Dominik Holek, PEF student (Open Informatics) and co-founder of reIS · updated 27 November 2026

I've opened UIS almost every week since my first year. Everything I need for my studies is in there. But finding it takes me longer than it should.

That's why a classmate and I started building reIS. Not because UIS doesn't work, but because it's built like a filing cabinet, not like an app for students.

What bothers me most about UIS

  • Names that tell you nothing. Course files live in the "List záznamníku učitele" (teacher's record sheet). Who would look there?
  • Everything is deep in the menus. Confirmation of study: Portál studenta › Podpora zvoleného studia › Tisk dokumentů › Tisk elektronicky pečetěných dokumentů.
  • It barely works on a phone. UIS is made for a computer. On a phone the timetable is a tiny table I have to zoom into.
  • What I learn moves again. In November 2026 UIS got a new design and some links moved. I had to look for things I knew by heart.
  • eduroam is a project of its own. Generate a certificate, download two files and install them on every device separately.

Where UIS is fine

What's in UIS is what counts. It's the official source and nothing replaces it. I still handle the application to study, a thesis or the course catalogue there, and I'm happy to, because it's all in one place.

What kind of student are you?

  • First-year in September: get reIS right away. In UIS you mainly need enrolment and your first login.
  • Just looking for an exam slot or course files: in reIS it's two taps.
  • Writing a thesis or dealing with the study office: that belongs in UIS.
My verdict: UIS is where the data lives. For everyday things, meaning the timetable, exams and files, I only open it through reIS now. I go to UIS when I need something official.

UIS MENDELU: frequently asked questions

How is reIS different from UIS?

reIS takes your data from UIS and shows it clearly: timetable, exams, courses, documents and eduroam in a few taps, on your phone and in your browser. It's made by MENDELU students, it isn't an official MENDELU app, and your data stays on your device.

How do I log in to UIS MENDELU?

On is.mendelu.cz, click Log in to the Personal administration of UIS and enter your login and password. UIS logs you out after a day of inactivity.

Forgot your UIS password: what now?

On the password reset page, enter your login and the e-mail you set in UIS for password recovery. You'll get a link. Never set up recovery? Write to uis@mendelu.cz.

Where do first-years get their UIS login and password?

The day after enrolment, log in to the e-application. The first table shows your UIS login and password. Then change the password and set up password recovery to your own e-mail.

More questions (8)

Who helps with UIS?

The system integrators at uis@mendelu.cz; for computers and accounts, helpdesk.mendelu.cz.

What can I find in UIS?

Study plans, the course catalogue, timetables, study materials, final theses, study financing and personal administration. The application to study is there too.

Where are the library and final theses in UIS?

On the is.mendelu.cz home page: the Library is under research information (Vědecko-výzkumné informace), final theses under study information (Studijní informace).

Does my UIS password work for eduroam Wi-Fi?

No. At MENDELU you sign in to eduroam with a certificate you generate in UIS. reIS sets it up for you.

Does my UIS login work for my university e-mail?

You sign in to Microsoft 365 (Outlook, Teams) as login@mendelu.cz.

Is UIS available in English?

Yes. Switch the language on is.mendelu.cz; the English version is at is.mendelu.cz/?lang=en.

How long do I keep UIS access after I finish?

Another 6 months. If you continue in a follow-on programme, access comes back by itself.

Where is the application to study at MENDELU?

The e-application is at is.mendelu.cz/prihlaska. It has its own login, which you get by e-mail after creating the application.
