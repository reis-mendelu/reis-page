MENDELU campus map

Mendel University in Brno by building, floor and room. Find Q01, the Aula in building A or a room in building Z (FRRMS) in seconds.

1Where do you want the map?

2Find the room

Calendar and Map are in the bottom bar.

  1. In Calendar, tap the pin next to the lesson.

  2. The map opens the building and floor and highlights the room.

  3. For any other room, type its code in Map, e.g. Z1.

Add reIS to Chrome

On Firefox? Get the Firefox extension. Then open Map in reIS.

  1. In reIS, click Map and type the room code, e.g. Q01.

  2. Pick the result: reIS switches building and floor and highlights the room.

You can also open the room from a course: click the room code next to the lesson.

Done! You know the building and the floor.

Where is room…?
  • The first letter is usually the building. Q01 is in building Q, A01 in building A, Z1 in building Z.
  • Buildings: A rectorate, B Faculty of Forestry and Wood Technology, C Faculty of AgriScience, Q Faculty of Business and Economics, E Institute of Lifelong Learning, X canteen, Z FRRMS.
  • The brackets in your timetable are the campus: ČP Brno – Černá Pole, Led Lednice.
  • Don't guess the floor from the number. Buildings count floors differently; reIS shows you the floor.
  • Floor plans with rooms in reIS cover buildings A, B, C, E, M, Q, X and Z. For most others (T or Lednice, say) it shows at least the building or campus.
