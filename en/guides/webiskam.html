WebISKAM at MENDELU: login and payments

WebISKAM is the SKM site for ISIC credit and dorm rent. You log in as you do in UIS. On your phone, the reIS app shows the same.

1Where do you want to open WebISKAM?

2Pay your dorm rent

  1. In the reIS app, open Profile › ISIC.

  2. The first time, sign in as you do in UIS. You type the password on MENDELU's own sign-in page; reIS never sees it.

  3. Tap Pay next to your rent.

Quickest on your phone. Scan the QR code, or:

Show the phone steps

Or on the web in WebISKAM (its labels are in Czech):

  1. Open webiskam.mendelu.cz, click Přihlásit (Log in) and sign in as you do in UIS.

  2. Credit: Účet › Konta › Nabít.

  3. Rent: Účet › Základní údaje (Basic details) › Uhradit (Pay).

That's it! Your ISIC credit and dorm rent are sorted.
Without reIS: the official way
  • Rent is paid by direct debit (inkaso), which you set up in the accommodation system.
  • Failed? Pay at once by card in WebISKAM or at the dorm reception by the 20th, or a 10 CZK/day penalty applies.

Accommodation for students (SKM, Czech)

Not working?
  • Pay is greyed out. Your ISIC credit (main account) is short of money. Tap Top up first.
  • I see no rent. You owe nothing; it's usually taken by direct debit.
  • Does reIS pay for me? No, you confirm the payment yourself in WebISKAM.
