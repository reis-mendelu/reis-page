WebISKAM at MENDELU: login and payments

WebISKAM is the SKM site where you top up your ISIC for the canteens and pay dorm rent. You log in with your UIS login and password. On your phone, reIS shows the same.

You'll find: ISIC credit · dorm rent and direct debit · the deposit and housing contract · your meal history.

1Where do you want to open WebISKAM?

2Check your credit and pay your rent

Profile is in the bottom bar. Pay only shows up when WebISKAM is asking you for something.

  1. In the reIS app, open Profile › ISIC.

  2. The first time, tap Show ISIC balance and sign in as you do in UIS.

  3. Under the balance you see your dorm rent and when it is due. Tap Pay.

  4. WebISKAM opens with its Uhradit (Pay) window. You confirm the payment with its button; reIS never pays anything itself.

Quickest on your phone. The reIS browser extension doesn't show WebISKAM; in the reIS app your credit and rent take a few taps. Scan the QR code, or:

Show the phone steps

Or on the web in WebISKAM (its labels are in Czech):

  1. Open webiskam.mendelu.cz, click Přihlásit (Log in) and sign in as you do in UIS.

  2. ISIC credit: Účet › Konta, then Nabít (Top up) next to Hlavní konto (the main account).

  3. Dorm rent: open Informace o klientovi (Client information) and click Uhradit (Pay) next to the overdue payment. It is paid from the main account, so top that up first.

That's it! In the canteen you pay with your ISIC from the topped-up account, and your rent is paid.
Without reIS: the official way
  1. Sign in at webiskam.mendelu.cz with Přihlásit (Log in) and your UIS login and password. It works in a phone browser too.
  2. ISIC credit: Účet › Konta, then Nabít next to Hlavní konto. ISIC top-up step by step
  3. Dorm rent is normally paid by direct debit (inkaso) every month. You set it up by signing in to the accommodation system and entering your bank account number.

According to SKM:

  • Size of the debit: accommodation, meals, internet and other services including VAT. It changes with the number of days in the month.
  • Debit failed: pay the amount at once. By card through the payment gateway in WebISKAM, or by card or cash at the dorm reception or the accommodation office.
  • Deadline: the 20th of the month. After that a contractual penalty of 10 CZK per day is added.
  • 5,000 CZK deposit: refundable; you can pay it online by card before reserving a room. In later years you don't pay it again, it carries over.
  • Contract: if you lived in the dorms last year, you sign it electronically in WebISKAM.

Accommodation for students (SKM, Czech)

Login or payment not working?
  • Where do I type my password? Straight into MENDELU's sign-in page, the same login and password as UIS. reIS never sees it.
  • WebISKAM signed me out. It does that after a while of inactivity. Sign in again.
  • Pay is greyed out. Rent is paid from the main account and it doesn't hold enough. reIS says how much is missing. Tap Top up, then pay.
  • Nothing happened after I paid. WebISKAM quietly reloads the page after a payment and tells you nothing. You know it went through when the payment disappears from the list.
  • I see no payments. You owe nothing. Rent is mostly taken by direct debit, so the list is usually empty.
  • Does reIS pay for me? No. You always confirm the payment yourself in the WebISKAM window.

Otherwise the SKM contacts; for housing, dormitory@mendelu.cz.
