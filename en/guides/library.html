MENDELU library

The MENDELU library (ÚVIS) has branches in building A on Zemědělská, in building Z and in Lednice. You can borrow books, print, scan and book a study room there.

How to book a study room, when the library is open and how to borrow a book.

1Book a study room in reIS

In the reIS phone app (iPhone, iPad, Android) it takes a few taps.

  1. In reIS, open the Map and search for "study rooms", or tap the study rooms pin by building A.

  2. Pick the day and the hours. Next to a free room, tap Book.

  3. You get a confirmation e-mail and the booking appears in your calendar. You can cancel it there with one tap.

  4. Collect the key at the loan desk; your ISIC is enough.

The library receives your name, e-mail, student number and faculty, the same as when you book on its website.

2Opening hours

  • Library A (building A, basement): Mon–Fri 7–19
  • Library IC (building A, basement): Mon–Thu 7–19, Fri 7–16
  • Library Z (building Z, 1st floor): Mon–Thu 8:30–18, Fri 8:30–16
  • Lednice library (ZF campus, building D): Mon–Thu 8–18, Fri 8–15
  • Evening study room (building A, basement): while the campus is open

According to uvis.mendelu.cz. Check temporary changes (holidays, for example) there.

MENDELU library: frequently asked questions

How do I book a study room at the MENDELU library?

In reIS: Map › study rooms › pick the hours › Book. Without reIS: pick a free slot in the calendar on uvis.mendelu.cz and you get a confirmation e-mail. Collect the key at the loan desk with your ISIC.

What are the MENDELU library opening hours?

Library A in building A is open on weekdays 7–19. Library Z Mon–Thu 8:30–18, Fri until 16; Lednice Mon–Thu 8–18, Fri until 15.

How do I borrow a book from the MENDELU library?

Register online in the catalogue at katalog.mendelu.cz: log in with your UIS login and password and register under My account. It is free for students and valid for the whole academic year. A loan lasts 30 days and you can renew it twice.

How much do printing and copying cost?

A4 black-and-white 1.50 CZK, A4 colour 6 CZK, double-sided costs twice as much. You pay from your ISKAM account with your ISIC. Scanning is free.

More questions (8)

What study rooms does the library have?

Library A has two individual rooms (up to 2 people), two team rooms (for 6 and for 10) and a seminar room for 10 to 18 people. Library IC has two rooms for 6. Each has a computer, sockets, Wi-Fi and most have a large screen.

How long can I book a study room for?

One booking is one hour; for longer, book several hours in a row. A booking is held for 15 minutes after it starts, then the library offers it to others. In reIS you can book at most 4 hours a day.

Where is the MENDELU library?

Library A and IC: Zemědělská 1, building A, basement. Library Z: tř. Generála Píky 7, building Z, 1st floor. Lednice: Valtická 337, ZF campus, building D, ground floor.

Is there Wi-Fi in the library?

Yes, eduroam covers the whole library. You log in to the library computers with your UIS login and password.

How do I return a book when the library is closed?

Use the book drop by building A or Z, or by building D in Lednice.

How do I reach the databases from home?

Through proxy.mendelu.cz, through the VPN, or for databases such as Scopus or Web of Science by signing in through your institution with your UIS login.

Can I use the library if I am not a MENDELU student?

Entry and studying on site are free for everyone. The public can borrow too; registration costs 240 CZK a year.

Who do I contact at the library?
