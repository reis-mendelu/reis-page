eduroam at MENDELU

eduroam is the Wi-Fi network of universities around the world. At MENDELU you sign in with a certificate from UIS, not a password.

A guide to connecting to the eduroam Wi-Fi at every MENDELU faculty (PEF, AF, LDF, ZF, FRRMS) in a minute, with no faro-free and no certificates to download.

1Where do you want eduroam?

2Set up eduroam

Mobile data is enough. On a Mac, reIS is also in the App Store.

  1. In the reIS app, open Profile › Eduroam.

  2. Tap Set up eduroam and confirm.

Add reIS to Chrome

On Firefox? Get the Firefox extension. Then open eduroam in reIS.

  1. Click Download eduroam profile and open the file.

  2. Open System Settings › Profiles.

  3. Double-click "MENDELU eduroam (reIS)" › Install.

  4. Enter the password reIS shows you.

Add reIS to Chrome

Install the geteduroam app once. Then open eduroam in reIS and click Download eduroam profile.

  1. In geteduroam, click ··· › Load .eap-config file…

  2. Open the downloaded eduroam-reis.eap-config.

  3. Click Next.

  4. Click Install next to the MENDELU certificate.

  5. Enter the password from reIS and click Connect.

That's it! You'll connect on campus automatically.

eduroam at MENDELU: frequently asked questions

What is the eduroam password at MENDELU?

You don't type one for the Wi-Fi; you sign in with a certificate. The only password is for installing the certificate: UIS shows it when you generate one, and reIS shows it in the app.

Why is eduroam not working?

  • Certificate expired. It lasts 366 days and IS never renews it. reIS warns you ahead of time.
  • Two certificates installed. Keep only the valid one.
  • iPhone won't download the certificate. Use Safari.
  • Android asks for a domain. mendelu.cz, no trailing space.
  • "Unable to join" off campus. That's fine: eduroam isn't in range.
  • Mendelu-host won't work. It's for guests only; students connect to eduroam.

Otherwise helpdesk.mendelu.cz or the official FAQ.

Where do I find the eduroam certificate in UIS?

Technologie a jejich správa › Certifikát bezdrátové sítě (eduroam) › Vygenerovat certifikát. Download the user and the root certificate. With reIS there is nothing to download.

How do I set up eduroam MENDELU on Android?

With reIS: Profile › Eduroam › Set up eduroam. By hand: method TLS, domain mendelu.cz, identity login@mendelu.cz (your own UIS login in place of “login”). Android needs a screen lock (PIN or password) before it allows the certificate.

More questions (10)

How do I set up eduroam without reIS (the official way)?

  1. Connect to faro-free and log in at is.mendelu.cz.
  2. In UIS, open the eduroam certificate page (Czech: Technologie a jejich správa › Certifikát bezdrátové sítě) and generate a certificate. Copy its password.
  3. Download the user and root certificates and install them:
  • iPhone and iPad: Safari only, mode EAP-TLS, identity login@mendelu.cz (your own UIS login in place of “login”). Guide
  • Android: method TLS, domain mendelu.cz, identity login@mendelu.cz (your own UIS login in place of “login”). Guide
  • Windows: root into Trusted Root Certification Authorities, user into Personal. Guide
  • Mac: both certificates into Keychain Access, Always Trust. Guide

How do I connect a Windows laptop or a MacBook to eduroam?

The reIS extension downloads a ready-made profile: on a Mac you open it in System Settings › Profiles, on Windows in the geteduroam app. By hand: on Windows the root certificate goes into Trusted Root Certification Authorities and the user one into Personal; on a Mac both go into Keychain Access, set to Always Trust.

How do I set up eduroam MENDELU on iPhone and iPad?

With reIS: Profile › Eduroam › Set up eduroam. By hand: download the certificate in Safari only, mode EAP-TLS, identity login@mendelu.cz (your own UIS login in place of “login”).

I have no mobile data. How do I get the certificate?

Join the faro-free network: MENDELU sites open there, UIS included, so you can download the certificate. Then switch to eduroam.

How long does the certificate last and how do I renew it?

366 days. Then generate a new one, install it and delete the old one from the device. Each device (phone, laptop, tablet) is set up separately.

Can eduroam get my account blocked?

Yes. If a device keeps trying to sign in with an expired certificate, the server blocks the account. Always delete an expired certificate.

Does MENDELU eduroam work at other universities?

Yes. With your MENDELU certificate you can connect at MUNI, VUT and universities abroad that have eduroam, for example on your Erasmus.

What are the faro-free and Mendelu-host networks for?

faro-free is only for downloading the certificate: you connect without one, but only MENDELU sites such as is.mendelu.cz open. Mendelu-host is for guests; students and staff use eduroam.

What if my device can't use a certificate?

Devices without certificate support can use the eduroam-peap network with a username and password. The helpdesk grants access by hand.

Who helps with eduroam at MENDELU?

The Department of Information Technology (OIT): helpdesk.mendelu.cz or the contacts at tech.mendelu.cz.
