eduroam at MENDELU: set up in 3 taps

eduroam Wi-Fi needs a one-year certificate from UIS. reIS fetches it and sets it up for you.

Checked on 15. 11. 2026 against eduroam.mendelu.cz. reIS is an independent student project, not an official MENDELU app.

iPhone, iPad and Android

No faro-free, nothing to download: mobile data is enough.

  1. In the reIS app, open Profile › Eduroam.

  2. Tap Set up eduroam and confirm. You'll connect on campus by yourself.

Mac

Open eduroam in the reIS extension for Chrome or Firefox.

  1. Click Download eduroam profile and open the file.

  2. Open System Settings › Profiles.

  3. Double-click "MENDELU eduroam (reIS)" › Install.

  4. Enter the certificate password reIS shows you.

Windows

Install the geteduroam app once, then click Download eduroam profile in the reIS extension.

  1. In geteduroam, click ··· › Load .eap-config file…

  2. Open the downloaded eduroam-reis.eap-config.

  3. On the MENDELU eduroam (reIS) profile, click Next.

  4. Click Install next to the MENDELU certificate.

  5. Enter the password from reIS and click Connect.

Without reIS: the official way

  1. Connect to faro-free and log in at is.mendelu.cz.
  2. Open the eduroam certificate page (Czech: Technologie a jejich správa › Certifikát bezdrátové sítě) and generate a certificate. Copy its password.
  3. Download the user and root certificates and install them for your device:
  • iPhone and iPad: Safari only, mode EAP-TLS, identity login@mendelu.cz. Guide
  • Android: method TLS, domain mendelu.cz, identity login@mendelu.cz. Guide
  • Windows: root into Trusted Root Certification Authorities, user into Personal. Guide
  • Mac: both certificates into Keychain Access, Always Trust. Guide

eduroam not working

  • Certificate expired. It lasts 366 days and IS never renews it. reIS warns you ahead of time.
  • Two certificates installed. Keep only the valid one.
  • iPhone won't download the certificate. Use Safari.
  • Android asks for a domain. mendelu.cz, no trailing space.
  • "Unable to join" off campus. That's fine: eduroam isn't in range.

Otherwise helpdesk.mendelu.cz or the official FAQ.
