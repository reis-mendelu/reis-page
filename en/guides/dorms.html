3How to get a place

  1. Right after you are admitted, email the dorm's accommodation office. Until 1 September you get a place from the first-year capacity.

  2. Pay the refundable 5,000 CZK deposit.

  3. Move in by Friday morning of the week teaching starts at the latest.

Details: in person, deposit, refunds
  • In person: go to the accommodation office by enrolment day at the latest, with an ID. You can pay the deposit by card.
  • After 1 September you only get a free bed. The first-year capacity lasts while it lasts, so write early.
  • Deposit back: full if you withdraw by 31 August, half by 15 September, nothing after that.
  • Not moved in on time: you lose the contract and the deposit.
  • The direct debit runs every month: the nightly price times the days in the month, plus the services you use.

What first-years ask
  • Is there Wi-Fi in the room? In JAK A, B, D, at Akademie and in the Taufer dorms, yes. In JAK C and in Lednice the room has only a network socket for a cable.
  • Cheapest of all? A triple room in Lednice at 116 CZK a night. In Brno, JAK block C at 126 CZK.
  • Bedding? You get it at the dorm's linen store and swap it there every 3 weeks.
  • Part-time (combined) study? The prices above are for full-time students; part-time students pay 280 CZK a night.
