2How to get a place

For first-years, academic year 2026/27, checked on 16. 11. 2026 against SKM's page for students and the accommodation schedule (both in Czech).

  1. Right after you are admitted, email the accommodation office of the dorm you want, or go there in person by enrolment day at the latest.

  2. Bring an ID and pay the refundable 5,000 CZK deposit, by card on the spot if you like.

  3. Write as early as you can, by 1. 9. 2026 at the latest. Until then you get a place from the capacity kept for first-years while it lasts, after that only a free bed.

  4. Set up a direct debit to SKM with a limit of at least 10,000 CZK. The rent is collected every month.

  5. If you change your mind: full deposit back until 31. 8., half until 15. 9., nothing after that.

  6. Move in by Friday morning of the week teaching starts at the latest. Otherwise you lose the contract and the deposit.

What first-years ask
  • Cheapest dorm in Brno? JAK block C: 126 CZK a night, 3,780 CZK for 30 nights. The cheapest of all is a triple room in Lednice at 116 CZK.
  • Where do I get my own bathroom? In JAK D and at Akademie. In the cells of JAK A, B and the Taufer dorms 2–3 rooms share one, in C it is on the floor.
  • Is there wifi in the room? In JAK A, B, D, at Akademie and in the Taufer dorms, yes. In JAK C and in Lednice the room has only a network socket for a cable.
  • What will I pay a month? The direct debit is the nightly price times the days in the month, plus the services you use (such as meals or internet).
  • Which dorm is closest? Akademie is inside building Z (FRRMS). To Zemědělská 1 it is a 17-minute walk from Akademie, 18 minutes from JAK and 37 minutes from the Taufer dorms.
  • Bedding? You get it at the dorm's linen store and swap it there every 3 weeks.
