Data & security

Your data stays on your device

reIS reads IS straight from your device. We don’t send your grades, timetable or files anywhere.

We never see your password.You sign in on the IS MENDELU login page itself. The app keeps only your signed-in session in your phone’s secure keychain, never your password.
Open source.All of reIS is public on GitHub, so anyone can check how it works. github.com/reis-mendelu

FAQ

What is reIS?
A clearer UIS MENDELU on your phone and in your browser. It reads your timetable, exams and grades straight from UIS MENDELU (is.mendelu.cz), and you sign in with your UIS account.
Is reIS an official MENDELU app?
No. reIS is an independent student project. UIS MENDELU and all the data in it belong to Mendel University.
Is the app in English?
Yes. It starts in Czech: pick English on the welcome screen, or later in Settings → Language. In the browser extension, it’s under Profile → Language.
Is reIS safe?
Your grades, timetable and files never leave your device, and the code is public. More under Data & security.
How much does reIS cost?
It’s free for students.
Where do I get reIS?
On the App Store, on Google Play and as an extension for Chrome and Firefox. Install guide: reisapp.cz/navod.
